ABOUT / SHADOW STUDY

A shadow is
a relationship.

A shadow cannot exist independently. It must depend on both the body and light. Without a body blocking the light, this particular shadow would not appear. Without light, there would be no shadow to see.

Who we are comes not only from our own choices, but also from family, friends, language, environment, and past experiences. The same person can reveal different sides in different environments, just as the same body casts different outlines under different kinds of light.

Perhaps the true self is not one image that never changes, but a kind of continuity that remains across all these changes.
